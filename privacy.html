Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

What this app is

Emad Uploader is a personal tool used by a single person (its owner) to publish the owner's own short videos to the owner's own TikTok account, after the owner approves each upload. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

What information the app handles

  • TikTok authorization tokens for the owner's own TikTok account, which TikTok issues when the owner approves access. They are used only to upload videos the owner has approved.
  • The video file and caption that the owner chooses to publish.

The app does not collect information about any other TikTok user, does not read or store anyone's followers, messages or personal data, and does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

How the information is used

Tokens, videos and captions are used only to upload the owner's approved video to the owner's TikTok account. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are kept in a private configuration file on computers controlled by the owner. They are not published and not shared. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, to publish them. The app does not sell or share information with any other third party.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the owner wants the connection to stay active. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's app permissions settings and can delete the stored tokens from their own computers, after which the app can no longer act on the account.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle information about anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
