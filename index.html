Emad Uploader

Emad Uploader is a small personal tool that helps its owner publish their own short videos to their own social media accounts, including TikTok.

The owner prepares a video and a caption, reviews them, and explicitly approves each upload. Only after that approval does the tool send the video to TikTok. It does not post anything on its own, and it is not offered to other people.
